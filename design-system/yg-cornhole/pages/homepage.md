# Homepage override

The homepage uses a sport-performance editorial system rather than the generated luxury/liquid-glass direction in `MASTER.md`. This override follows the client brief and supplied product assets.

## Visual language

- Midnight `#0D151E`: header, product arena, and primary dark sections
- Competition navy `#142535`: equipment cards and trust surfaces
- Graphite `#283440`: secondary surfaces
- Steel `#AEB6BD`: dividers and material-inspired supporting details
- Mist `#E8ECEF`: product and information surfaces
- Signal red `#A73136`: announcement, calls to action, and match-day emphasis
- Bright red `#DF5A5D`: dark-surface labels and accessible focus state

## Type

- Display: narrow, heavy athletic grotesk treatment for campaign headlines
- Body: open humanist sans for long-form readability
- Utility: mono face for speed data, labels, and product specifications

## Layout and signature

The homepage follows a direct specialist-retail sequence: announcement, category-led navigation, product-first bag-wall hero, trust strip, visual category tiles, featured bag arena, education, brand story, custom-board feature, reassurance, and newsletter. The page uses a dark navy, brushed-steel, and signal-red rhythm taken directly from the supplied YG Bags logo. Its signature moment remains the pinned GSAP throwing lane where a bag tracks from control to finish as the user scrolls; this section and animation are preserved from the first concept.

The hero uses the client-supplied background-removed bag wall on the left side of a controlled navy court grid, leaving an uninterrupted copy field on the right. Hero display type uses a readable `.92` line-height plus explicit inter-line spacing instead of the earlier compressed overlap. Product cards use a wide 1.62:1 stage in a two-column arena layout so bag art remains large and uncropped. Client-supplied board, apparel, hoodie, and award cutouts carry the same product-first treatment deeper into the page.

## Rules

- Avoid glassmorphism, generic gradient cards, decorative pills, and stock athletic imagery that is not specifically cornhole-related.
- Use no more than one pinned section.
- Keep all primary targets at least 44px and preserve visible keyboard focus.
- Disable smooth scrolling and scripted motion for `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Verify at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 widths with no horizontal overflow.
